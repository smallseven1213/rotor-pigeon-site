01 — PRIVACY

隱私權政策

生效日期：2026 年 11 月 16 日

《旋頭鴿 ROTOR PIGEON》（以下稱「本遊戲」）由 7 Studio 製作。本遊戲免費遊玩，靠廣告維持。我們自己沒有伺服器，也不會收到你的個人資料；廣告由 Google AdMob 提供，Google 會依下方說明收集資料。

我們收集什麼

我們自己什麼都不收集。本遊戲沒有帳號、沒有登入，也沒有我們自己的分析或追蹤工具，遊玩時不會連上我們的任何伺服器。

廣告（Google AdMob）

每一輪的前 3 次接關免費，之後想接關可以選擇看一則廣告；買了「免廣告」就不再載入任何廣告。廣告由 Google AdMob 提供，為了顯示與衡量廣告，Google 的軟體會收集：裝置識別碼（廣告識別碼 IDFA 只有在你允許追蹤時才會取得）、由 IP 位址推估的大略位置、看過與點過的廣告、App 內的互動（例如開啟 App、觀看影片），以及當機與效能資料。這些資料可能被用於第三方廣告與分析，在你允許時也可能用於追蹤。Google 如何使用這些資料，請看 Google 在合作夥伴網站與應用程式中的資料使用方式 與 Google 隱私權政策。

遊戲裡也會出現我們自製的廣告（7 Studio 其他 App 的介紹）。自製廣告不收集任何資料；只有在你點「了解更多」時才會打開 App Store、Google Play 或該 App 的網站。

你的選擇

iPhone / iPad 第一次開啟時會詢問是否允許追蹤；拒絕後仍會有廣告，但不會用廣告識別碼做個人化，之後也可以在「設定 > 隱私權與安全性 > 追蹤」更改。Android 可以在系統設定重設或刪除廣告 ID。在歐洲經濟區、英國與瑞士，開啟時會先顯示 Google 的同意畫面。

存在裝置上的資料

最高分、語言與音效等設定、看過幾則廣告、以及是否已購買「免廣告」只存在你的裝置上。刪除 App 就會一併刪除。

購買

「免廣告」的購買由 Apple 的 App Store（Android 為 Google Play）處理。我們不會收到你的姓名、電子郵件或付款資料；App 只會向商店查詢「這個帳號是否已購買免廣告」。

兒童

本遊戲不以兒童為主要對象。我們自己不向任何人（包含兒童）收集個人資料；廣告相關的資料由 Google 依上方說明處理。

政策更新

如果這份政策有變動，會更新在這個頁面並修改生效日期。

聯絡

Privacy Policy

Effective 16 November 2026

ROTOR PIGEON ("the game") is made by 7 Studio. The game is free to play and paid for by ads. We run no server of our own and never receive your personal data; the ads come from Google AdMob, and Google collects data as described below.

What we collect

Nothing ourselves. The game has no account, no login and no analytics or tracking tools of our own, and it does not connect to any server of ours while you play.

Ads (Google AdMob)

The first 3 continues of every run are free; after that you may choose to watch an ad to continue. Buy "No Ads" and no ad is loaded again. The ads come from Google AdMob. To show and measure them, Google's software collects: device identifiers (the advertising identifier, IDFA, only if you allow tracking), approximate location estimated from your IP address, the ads you saw and tapped, interactions in the app (such as launching it or watching a video), and crash and performance data. This data may be used for third-party advertising and analytics, and, if you allow it, for tracking. How Google uses it: How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services and the Google Privacy Policy.

The game also shows our own house ads (other 7 Studio apps). House ads collect nothing; only tapping "Learn more" opens the App Store, Google Play or that app's website.

Your choices

On iPhone / iPad the game asks once whether you allow tracking. If you decline you still see ads, but the advertising identifier is not used to personalise them; you can change this later in Settings > Privacy & Security > Tracking. On Android you can reset or delete your advertising ID in the system settings. In the EEA, the UK and Switzerland, Google's consent screen appears first.

Data kept on your device

Your high score, settings such as language and sound, how many ads you have watched, and whether you bought "No Ads" are kept on your device only. Deleting the app deletes them.

Purchases

The "No Ads" purchase is handled by Apple's App Store (Google Play on Android). We do not receive your name, email address or payment details; the app only asks the store whether this account owns "No Ads".

Children

The game is not directed at children. We do not collect personal data from anyone, including children; ad-related data is handled by Google as described above.

Changes

If this policy changes, this page and its effective date will be updated.

Contact

Questions about this policy: open an issue on GitHub.
