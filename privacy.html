01 — PRIVACY

隱私權政策

生效日期：2026 年 11 月 16 日

《旋頭鴿 ROTOR PIGEON》（以下稱「本遊戲」）由 7 Studio 製作。本遊戲不收集、不儲存於伺服器、也不分享任何個人資料。

我們收集什麼

什麼都不收集。本遊戲沒有帳號、沒有登入、沒有廣告、沒有分析或追蹤工具，遊玩時也不會連上我們的任何伺服器。

存在裝置上的資料

最高分、語言與音效等設定、以及「已解鎖完整版」的標記只存在你的裝置上。刪除 App 就會一併刪除。

購買

完整版的購買由 Apple 的 App Store 處理。我們不會收到你的姓名、電子郵件或付款資料；App 只會向 App Store 查詢「這個 Apple 帳號是否已購買完整版」。

兒童

本遊戲不向任何人（包含兒童）收集個人資料。

政策更新

如果這份政策有變動，會更新在這個頁面並修改生效日期。

聯絡

對這份政策有疑問，請到 GitHub Issues 提出。

Privacy Policy

Effective 16 November 2026

ROTOR PIGEON ("the game") is made by 7 Studio. The game does not collect personal data, does not store any on a server and does not share any.

What we collect

Nothing. The game has no account, no login, no ads and no analytics or tracking tools, and it does not connect to any server of ours while you play.

Data kept on your device

Your high score, settings such as language and sound, and a flag saying the full game is unlocked are kept on your device only. Deleting the app deletes them.

Purchases

The full-game purchase is handled by Apple's App Store. We do not receive your name, email address or payment details; the app only asks the App Store whether this Apple Account owns the full game.

Children

The game does not collect personal data from anyone, including children.

Changes

If this policy changes, this page and its effective date will be updated.

Contact

Questions about this policy: open an issue on GitHub.
