02 — SUPPORT

客服與常見問題

遇到問題或有建議，請到 GitHub Issues 開一則回報，並附上裝置型號、系統版本與發生的情況（可附截圖）。

怎麼玩？

按住螢幕飛起、放開下降，點或拖畫面左右兩側閃避。沿著瓜子飛最安全。

為什麼會被貓抓、被雷打？

在一般地面停太久，貓會來；貼著畫面最上方飛太久，雷雲會來。離開地面或降低高度就沒事。

為什麼要看廣告？

遊戲免費，每一輪的前 3 次接關不用看廣告；之後想接關，可以選擇看一則廣告，不想看就回到標題重新開始。

怎麼去掉廣告？換手機怎麼辦？

標題畫面、暫停選單或接關畫面選「免廣告」，一次購買就永久沒有廣告、接關不用再看廣告。換裝置或重新安裝後，在同一個畫面按「恢復購買」即可，不會再扣款。

我的紀錄存在哪裡？

最高分與設定存在你的裝置上，刪除 App 會一併刪除。

Support & FAQ

Problems or ideas? Open an issue on GitHub with your device model, system version and what happened (screenshots help).

How do I play?

Hold the screen to climb, release to sink, tap or drag the left / right side to steer. The trail of seeds is the safe line.

Why did a cat or lightning get me?

Stay on a safe floor too long and the cat comes; hug the top of the screen too long and the thundercloud comes. Leave the ground or fly lower.

Why are there ads?

The game is free. The first 3 continues of every run need no ad; after that you may watch one ad to continue, or go back to the title and start again.

How do I remove the ads? What about a new phone?

Choose "NO ADS" on the title screen, the pause menu or the continue screen: one purchase removes every ad for good, and continues no longer need an ad. On a new device or after reinstalling, press "RESTORE PURCHASE" on the same screen; you are not charged again.

Where is my record kept?

Your high score and settings are on your device. Deleting the app deletes them.
